I am an incoming M.Sc. student in Computer Science at the University of British Columbia, advised by [Prof. Xin Tang](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/). My research interests are in applied machine learning and deep learning, with a focus on AI for healthcare and biological applications, and on building models that are interpretable and explainable.

Before UBC, I completed my B.B.A. in Information Management (minor in Computer Science and Information Engineering) at National Taiwan University, where I worked on ECG signal processing, indoor positioning, and speech LLMs across several labs. I also spent time as a Mitacs Globalink research intern at the University of Alberta and as an AI R&D intern at [Tomofun](https://furbo.com/us).
