I am an incoming M.Sc. student in Computer Science at the University of British Columbia, advised by [Prof. Xin Tang](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/). My research interests are in applied machine learning and deep learning, with a focus on AI for healthcare and biological applications, and on building models that are interpretable and explainable.

Before UBC, I completed my B.B.A. in Information Management (minor in Computer Science and Information Engineering) at National Taiwan University. There, I was an undergraduate research student in the Pervasive and Autonomous Intelligence Lab, advised by Prof. Fang-Jing Wu, working on indoor positioning from ambient light sensing, and a project research assistant in the Speech Processing and Machine Learning Lab, advised by Prof. Hung-yi Lee, working on Taiwan-localized speech LLMs. I was also a Mitacs Globalink research intern at the University of Alberta, working with Prof. Russell Greiner and Prof. Sunil Kalmady Vasu on deep survival prediction for cardiovascular patients.

On the industry side, I was an AI R&D intern at [Tomofun](https://furbo.com/us), a pet technology startup, where I worked on algorithm optimization and text-video retrieval.
